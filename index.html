Electron enrichment of interfacial Pt6 clusters on SrTiO3 from constrained density-functional theory

Fixed-geometry CDFT charge scan (GPAW) · report generated 29 September 2026

Abstract. Constrained DFT (CDFT) is used to add a controlled excess of electrons to the six-atom Pt region on relaxed SrTiO3–Pt models (pristine and interfacial oxygen vacancy; vacuum, water, and KOH environments). The natural CDFT charge of Pt6, Q0, is measured for each structure; the net charge is then scanned as Q = Q0 − Δn with Δn = 0, 0.5, 1.0, 1.5 and 2.0. At Δn = 0 the Lagrange multiplier vanishes and CDFT recovers unconstrained DFT. Energies and multipliers as a function of Δn quantify the cost of storing excess charge on Pt.

Methods (brief)

Geometries were taken from prior PBE+U(Ti) / DFT-D3 relaxations (fmax = 0.05 eV Å−1). CDFT calculations used GPAW (finite-difference, h = 0.15 Å, spin-polarized, dipole correction) with a Hirshfeld-style weight on the six Pt atoms. The constraint is the ionic net charge Q = Z − Ne on that region; Δn is the number of extra electrons relative to the unconstrained ground state. The total slab remains charge-neutral: excess electrons on Pt are drawn from the oxide / solvent region.
