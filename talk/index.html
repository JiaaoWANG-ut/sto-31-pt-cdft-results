K+ 会把电子留在 Pt 上吗Does K+ keep the extra electrons on Pt?

看电荷落在哪，以及 K+ 让它便宜了多少。Where the charge sits, and how much K+ lowers the energy.

做法The calculation

  • 原子停在基态结构上，只把电子约束到 Pt6。Atoms stay at the ground-state geometry. Only the electrons are constrained onto Pt6.
  • 从 0 加到 2 个电子。用 Bader 看它们落在哪个原子。Add 0 to 2 electrons. Bader charges show which atom receives them.
  • 用静电势估计每个离子的能量贡献。The electrostatic potential gives each ion’s energy contribution.

可以怎么说What can be said

  • 可以说：K+ 是反离子，能稳定 Pt 上的负电荷。You can say: K+ is a counter-ion and stabilizes the negative charge on Pt.
  • 不能说：所以 KOH 比纯水更能把电子留在 Pt 上。You cannot say: therefore KOH holds electrons on Pt better than pure water.
  • 因为模型里 Pt 上的 OH- 把 K+ 抵消了，总充电能和纯水几乎一样。OH- on Pt cancels K+. The total charging energy matches pure water.
